EUDI Standards Graph

How the STS Roadmap, the CIR Registry and the Errata Registry link to each other, built from the public issues in eudi-doc-standards-and-technical-specifications. Three views of the same data.

A. Network mapthe whole landscape at once B. CIR coverage matrixwhich CIR cites which standard C. Focus navigatorone item and its neighbours
Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a node to focus on it
Columns are CIRs in the CIR Registry, oldest left. A filled cell means the standard carries that CIR's label. Red marks a standard with a published erratum.
Click any neighbour to move the focus to it
Neighbours sit in sectors by registry and direction: arrows point from the issue that holds the link to the one it links to (a CIR points to the standards it cites, an erratum to the standard it corrects). Standards are split into “references” and “referenced by”. Each focus has its own link, e.g. #focus-284.

Which view to use

A. Network map overview

See the whole landscape and its clusters, such as the large CIR 2024/2977 and 2026/1731 hubs. Click any node to bring its neighbours around it; click the background to return to the full map.

B. CIR coverage matrix analysis

Check exactly which standards each CIR cites. Every link is a cell, gaps show as empty rows or columns, and standards with an erratum are marked in red.

C. Focus navigator navigation

Start from one standard, CIR or erratum and move from neighbour to neighbour. Every item has its own link, ready to paste into an issue or a registry entry.
